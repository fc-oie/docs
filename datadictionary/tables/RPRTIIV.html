RPRTIIV

Below are the column names for RPRTIIV.

Columns for RPRTIIV
COLUMN_NAME DATA_TYPE DATA_LENGTH COMMENTS
RPRTIIV_AIDY_CODE VARCHAR2 16 AID YEAR CODE: The financial aid processing year for which information will be displayed on the form, report, and/or process.
RPRTIIV_PIDM NUMBER 22 IDENTIFICATION NUMBER: The identification number of the student that is to be displayed on the form, report, and/or process.
RPRTIIV_TERM_CODE VARCHAR2 24 TERM CODE: The term code of the student’s withdrawal.
RPRTIIV_USER_ID VARCHAR2 120 USER ID: The user ID of the person who inserted or last updated this record.
RPRTIIV_ACTIVITY_DATE DATE 7 ACTIVITY DATE: The date that information in this record was entered or last updated.
RPRTIIV_SYS_IND VARCHAR2 4 SYSTEM INDICATOR: Indicates whether changes were made manually or by a process.
RPRTIIV_SEQ_NO NUMBER 22 FINANCIAL AID SEQUENCE NUMBER: The sequence number of the Return of Title IV Funds calculation.
RPRTIIV_WDRL_SEQ_NO NUMBER 22 STUDENT WITHDRAWAL SEQUENCE NUMBER: The sequence number linking the student’s withdrawal record in Student to the corresponding Title IV Financial Aid calculation.
RPRTIIV_COMMENT VARCHAR2 960 RETURN OF TITLE IV FUNDS CALCULATION COMMENT: Information that has been entered concerning a particular term concerning the Return of Title IV Funds calculation.
RPRTIIV_SIM_MODE_IND VARCHAR2 4 RETURN OF TITLE IV FUNDS CALCULATION SIMULATION MODE INDICATOR: The Return of Title IV Funds Calculation Simulation Indicator indicates if the calculation has been run in simulation mode or not. Valid values are Y and Null.
RPRTIIV_TITLE_IV_FUND_CODE VARCHAR2 24 RETURN OF TITLE IV FUNDS FUND CODE: The fund code that is to be included in the Return of Title IV Funds Calculation.
RPRTIIV_ELIG_DISB_AMT NUMBER 22 STUDENT’S ELIGIBLE DISBURSEMENT AMOUNT BY FUND: The total of the student’s eligible Title IV disbursement by fund for the term or the period of enrollment. This amount is the total of what has been disbursed plus what could have been disbursed.
RPRTIIV_DISB_AMT NUMBER 22 STUDENT’S DISBURSEMENT AMOUNT BY FUND: The total of the student’s Title IV disbursements by fund for the term or the period of enrollment.
RPRTIIV_UNDISB_AMT NUMBER 22 STUDENT’S ELIGIBLE NON-DISBURSED TITLE IV AWARDS BY FUND: The total of the student’s eligible awards by fund that has not yet been disbursed to the student’s account for the term or period of enrollment.
RPRTIIV_INST_REQ_RET_AMT NUMBER 22 INSTITUTION’S REQUIRED RETURN AMOUNT BY FUND: The amount that the institution is required to return by fund for the term or the period of enrollment.
RPRTIIV_INST_OPTL_RET_AMT NUMBER 22 INSTITUTION’S OPTIONAL RETURN AMOUNT BY FUND: The total amount that the institution is optionally returning to the appropriate Title IV program on the student’s behalf for the term or period of enrollment.
RPRTIIV_STU_REQ_RET_AMT NUMBER 22 STUDENT’S RESPONSIBILITY OF RETURN BY FUND: The amount that the student is responsible to return to the appropriate Title IV fund program for the term or period of enrollment.
RPRTIIV_LATE_DISB_AMT NUMBER 22 STUDENT’S POST-WITHDRAWAL DISBURSEMENT AMOUNT BY FUND: The total amount by fund that the institution must disburse or offer to disburse of earned aid that could have been disbursed prior to the student’s withdrawal but was not.
RPRTIIV_REV_AWARD_AMT NUMBER 22 REVISED AWARD AMOUNT BY FUND: The student’s revised award amount due to the Return of Title IV Funds calculation.
RPRTIIV_FUND_LOCK_IND VARCHAR2 4 RETURN OF TITLE IV FUNDS LOCK INDICATOR: This field is checked to prevent changes during the Return of Title IV Funds Calculation. Allows the user to lock funds on a fund by fund basis to ensure that they do not change when the calculation is run.
RPRTIIV_CALC_LOCK_IND VARCHAR2 4 RETURN OF TITLE IV FUNDS CALCULATION LOCK INDICATOR: This field will be populated when a Return of Title IV Funds Calculation is performed. The lock can be unchecked in order to perform another calculation for the same term.
RPRTIIV_TITLE_IV_EARNED_AMT NUMBER 22 TITLE IV AID EARNED: The total Title IV aid earned by the student.
RPRTIIV_TITLE_IV_UNEARNED_AMT NUMBER 22 TITLE IV AID UNEARNED: The total Title IV aid unearned by the student.
RPRTIIV_REM_AID_DISB_AMT NUMBER 22 TITLE IV AID REMAINING TO BE DISBURSED TO THE STUDENT: The total eligible Title IV financial aid remaining to be disbursed to the student.
RPRTIIV_AID_RET_AMT NUMBER 22 THE TOTAL TITLE IV AID TO BE RETURNED: The total Title IV aid to be returned by the student and the institution.
RPRTIIV_INST_REQ_RET_SHARE NUMBER 22 INSTITUTION’S REQUIRED SHARE OF RETURN: The total share of Title IV aid required to be returned by the institution.
RPRTIIV_STU_REQ_RET_SHARE NUMBER 22 STUDENT’S REQUIRED SHARE OF RETURN: The total share of Title IV aid to be returned by the student.
RPRTIIV_SURROGATE_ID NUMBER 22 SURROGATE ID: Immutable unique key
RPRTIIV_VERSION NUMBER 22 VERSION: Optimistic lock token.
RPRTIIV_DATA_ORIGIN VARCHAR2 120 DATA ORIGIN: Source system that created or updated the data.
RPRTIIV_VPDI_CODE VARCHAR2 24 VPDI CODE: Multi-entity processing code.
RPRTIIV_SCPS_CODE VARCHAR2 24 STUDENT CENTRIC PERIOD CODE: The Student Centric Period code of the withdrawal.
