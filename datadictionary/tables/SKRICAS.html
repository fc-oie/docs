SKRICAS_EXP_ENL_END_DT DATE 7 EXPECTED ENROLMENT END DATE: This field holds the last date by which the student is expected to have completed enrolment for the program.
SKRICAS_NS_NOTES VARCHAR2 4000 NO SHOW NOTES: This field holds notes entered by the user when the student does not enrol by the expected date.
