SZRTREQ_TRANSCRIPT_CREATED DATE 7 Date when the transcript was created.

SZRTREQ_CERTIFICATION VARCHAR2 8 RCL27 - Certification Request. Release transcript after this certification is received. GE (GE cert), IG (IGETC cert), or BO (both).
SZRTREQ_SIGNATURE_VOUCHER VARCHAR2 8 RCL31 - Student Signature Voucher. Indicates if the requestor has explicit authorization from the student. S (Signed request) or N (No signature).

SZRTREQ_REQ_PHONE_EXT VARCHAR2 20 REQ26 - Telephone number extension for contacting the requestor.
SZRTREQ_REQ_MAIL_ADDR_STREET VARCHAR2 160 REQ31 - Street address or PO Box portion of the requestor’s mailing address.

SZRTREQ_STU_MIDDLE_NAME VARCHAR2 80 STU13 - Student’s middle name or initial at the transcript institution.
SZRTREQ_STU_SUFFIX VARCHAR2 12 STU14 - Suffix to the student’s name at the transcript institution.
