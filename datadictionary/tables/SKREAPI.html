Below are the column names for SKREAPI.

Columns for SKREAPI
COLUMN_NAME DATA_TYPE DATA_LENGTH COMMENTS
SKREAPI_PIDM NUMBER 22 PIDM: Internal identification number of the person.
SKREAPI_ID NUMBER 22 ID: The record ID on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_ECAF_REQ_TYPE VARCHAR2 120 ECAF REQUEST TYPE: Request Type from the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_SEQNO VARCHAR2 80 SEQUENCE NUMBER: Unique Record Identifier within Banner.
SKREAPI_STUDENT_ID VARCHAR2 36 STUDENT ID: Student Identifier as recorded on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_HELP_TYPE VARCHAR2 4 HELP TYPE: The type of Commonwealth Assistance that is requested.
SKREAPI_ACTIVITY_DATE DATE 7 ACTIVITY DATE: Date when the record was created or last updated.
SKREAPI_USER_ID VARCHAR2 120 USER ID: User ID of the user who created or last updated the record.
SKREAPI_VERSION NUMBER 22 VERSION: One up, start at 0 increment with each update.
SKREAPI_SURROGATE_ID NUMBER 22 SURROGATE ID: Populated from sequence
SKREAPI_SUB_DATE DATE 7 SUBMITTED DATE: Date when record was submitted to the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_MOD_DATE DATE 7 MODIFIED DATE: Date when record was modified to the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_PROCESS_IND VARCHAR2 4 PROCESS INDICATOR: Indicates whether eCAF has been processed on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_REV_IND VARCHAR2 4 REVISION INDICATOR: Indicates whether the record has been revised on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_INIT_MAIL VARCHAR2 512 INITIAL EMAIL ADDRESS: Initial email address recorded on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_PERS_MAIL VARCHAR2 512 PERSONAL EMAIL ADDRESS: Personal email address recorded on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_LAST_NAME VARCHAR2 240 LAST NAME: Last name of the student recorded on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_FIRST_NAME VARCHAR2 240 GIVEN NAME: Given name of the student recorded on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_DOB DATE 7 BIRTH DATE: Birth Date of the student recorded on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_GENDER VARCHAR2 4 GENDER CODE: Gender code of a person recorded on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_COURSE_CODE VARCHAR2 80 COURSE OF STUDY CODE: Course of Study Code recorded on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_COURSE_NAME VARCHAR2 800 COURSE OF STUDY NAME: The full name of the Course of Study recorded on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_CAMP_DESC VARCHAR2 800 CAMPUS DESCRIPTION: The name of the campus recorded on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_TFN RAW 200 TAX FILE NUMBER: The validated and encrypted Tax File Number recorded on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_ADD_DATA VARCHAR2 1200 ADDITIONAL DATA: Additional Data for the provider to store with this record.
SKREAPI_JOBID VARCHAR2 120 JOB ID: One Up Number of the job.
SKREAPI_JOBNAME VARCHAR2 120 JOB NAME: Indicates the name of the process.
SKREAPI_TFN_APPLIED VARCHAR2 4 TFN APPLIED: Indicates whether student has applied for a Tax File Number.
SKREAPI_FORMCODE VARCHAR2 120 FORM CODE: The Form code as recorded on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_CREATION_DATE DATE 7 CREATION DATE: Date when the record was created on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_EXPIRY_DATE DATE 7 EXPIRY DATE: Expiry Date of the record on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_ECAF_STATUS VARCHAR2 120 ECAF STATUS: The status of the eCAF on the Australian government website.
SKREAPI_SKRECAF_SUCCESS VARCHAR2 4 SUCCESS: Indicates whether the record was inserted into SKRECAF successfully
SKREAPI_REVISION_DUE_DATE DATE 7 REVISION DUE DATE: The date the record is due for the revision on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_DELETE_REASON VARCHAR2 1020 DELETE REASON: Reason the record is deleted on the Australian government eCAF system.
SKREAPI_ORG_PIDM NUMBER 22 ORGANISATION PIDM: The unique identifier for the organisation.
SKREAPI_OS_STUDY_TYPE VARCHAR2 120 OS STUDY TYPE: The type of OS HELP student has requested.
SKREAPI_OS_HELP_AMOUNT NUMBER 22 OS HELP AMOUNT: Amount student has requested for OS HELP.
SKREAPI_OS_STUDY_COMM_DATE DATE 7 OS STUDY COMMENCEMENT DATE: Commencement Date of Overseas study.
SKREAPI_OS_HELP_SUPP_AMOUNT NUMBER 22 SUPPLEMENTARY AMOUNT: Supplementary language study amount.
SKREAPI_ASIAN_LANG_COMM_DATE DATE 7 LANGUAGE COMMENCEMENT DATE: Language study commencement date.
SKREAPI_PROGRESS_OUTCOME VARCHAR2 120 PROGRESSION OUTCOME: Outcome of student progression submission.
SKREAPI_PROGRESS_DUE_DATE DATE 7 PROGRESSION DUE DATE: Due date for student progression submission.
SKREAPI_PROGRESS_NUMBER NUMBER 22 PROGRESSION NUMBER: Student progression submission number.
SKREAPI_ECAF_ID NUMBER 22 ECAF ID: The Australian government unique identifier for this eCAF.
SKREAPI_DATA_ORIGIN VARCHAR2 120 DATA ORIGIN: Source system that created or updated the row.
SKREAPI_VPDI_CODE VARCHAR2 24 VPDI CODE: Code of institution to which the data belongs.
SKREAPI_STUDY_AUST_IND VARCHAR2 4 STUDY AUSTRALIA INDICATOR: An indicator of whether the student is resident in Australia for the duration of study. Default value is Y.
SKREAPI_CONTRIBUTION_METHOD VARCHAR2 80 CONTRIBUTION METHOD: The Contribution Method is used to know whether the student should be put on a HECS HELP contract or not.
SKREAPI_PART_PAYMENT_IND VARCHAR2 4 PARTIAL PAYMENT INDICATOR: An indicator of whether or not the student intends to make partial HECS HELP payments. Default value is N.
SKREAPI_NATN_CODE_PRIM VARCHAR2 20 NATION CODE PRIMARY: Primary nation for Overseas Study HELP.
SKREAPI_NATN_CODE_SECD VARCHAR2 20 NATION CODE SECONDARY: Secondary nation for Overseas Study HELP.
SKREAPI_LANG_CODE VARCHAR2 16 LANGUAGE CODE: Overseas Study HELP Language code.
SKREAPI_OTHER_REASON VARCHAR2 1200 OTHER REASON: Other basis for assessment of students academic suitability.
SKREAPI_STUD_ACAD_SUIT VARCHAR2 100 STUDENT ACADEMIC SUITABILITY: Basis for assessment of students academic suitability.
SKREAPI_CSP_IND VARCHAR2 4 COMMONWEALTH SUPPORTED PLACE INDICATOR: An indicator of whether student only has a Commonwealth supported place and not any HECS HELP. Default value is N.
SKREAPI_STARTUP_HELP_AMOUNT NUMBER 22 STARTUP HELP AMOUNT: Amount student has requested for Startup HELP.
